Welcome to The Garden Spa Room

A calm space

The Garden Spa Room is a North East Melbourne wellness space built around thoughtful treatments, peaceful surroundings and personalised care.

From massage and beauty therapy to Reiki and Japanese-inspired treatments, our aim is to create a peaceful experience where you can slow down, reconnect and enjoy a moment for yourself.

Every detail is inspired by the natural world, creating a welcoming atmosphere where relaxation comes first.
